Project allocation

Partner project with Ayan Muday for CO3105. We built it closely together.

Staff propose final-year projects. Each member of staff has a supervision limit and subject areas. Each project belongs to one subject and can be taken by a set number of students. Each student ranks up to four projects.

The program gives each student the highest choice that still has space. Supervisors are then assigned in three steps: the person who proposed the project, then someone in that subject, then anyone who still has room.

A small example

Four students, four software projects, and one place on each. Everyone gets their first choice, supervised by the person who proposed it. The score is 32.
